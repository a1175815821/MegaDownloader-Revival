# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds this project:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit permission
- Using issues or discussions to distribute pirated content, malware or links to illegal material

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews — and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour using GitHub’s reporting option on the relevant content or profile. GitHub handles these reports under its own process.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and will explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

Consequences escalate with severity and repetition:

1. A private warning, with clarification of why the behaviour was inappropriate
2. A temporary ban from interaction with the project
3. A permanent ban from the project
